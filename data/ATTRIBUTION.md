# Data attribution

Two files in this directory are **real open data**. Everything else in the demo —
every KPI reading, subscriber count, ringgit and trouble ticket — is synthetic,
generated by `backend/seed/seed_all.py` from a fixed random seed.

---

## `my_states.geojson` — state and federal-territory boundaries

**Source:** [geoBoundaries](https://www.geoboundaries.org/), gbOpen release,
Malaysia ADM1.

**License:** [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) (CC BY 4.0).

> Runfola, D. et al. (2020) geoBoundaries: A global database of political
> administrative boundaries. *PLoS ONE* 15(4): e0231866.
> <https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0231866>

**What was changed:** properties reduced to `state_code`, `name` and
`region_group`; "Malacca" and "Penang" renamed to Melaka and Pulau Pinang;
coordinates rounded to five decimal places. Rebuild with `python data/build_geo.py`.

---

## `towers_my.csv` — cell-site positions

**Source:** [OpenCelliD](https://opencellid.org/), the `cell_towers` dataset as
hosted on the [ClickHouse public playground](https://play.clickhouse.com/),
filtered to MCC 502 (Malaysia).

**License:** [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) (CC BY-SA 4.0).

> Cell tower data © OpenCelliD contributors, licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0.

**What was changed:** cells deduplicated onto a ~100 m grid so that co-located
cells collapse into one physical mast; a deterministic uniform sample of 4,000
masts taken; columns reduced to latitude, longitude, radio generation and cell
count. Rebuild with `python data/build_towers.py`.

**Because CC BY-SA is a share-alike license**, this file and any derivative of it
carry that obligation. It is used here only to place synthetic sites at
plausible real coordinates; nothing in the generated database is derived from
OpenCelliD beyond position and radio type.

If `build_towers.py` cannot reach the playground, it writes nothing and the seed
falls back to synthesizing positions around real city centroids. The demo works
either way; the map is just less convincing.

## Everything else is invented

**Nusatel is not a real company.** Its network, subscribers, revenue, costs,
alarms and tickets are generated data designed to be plausible and to contain
four specific story arcs the demo script walks through. Do not cite any figure
in this repository as a fact about Malaysian telecommunications.

Benchmarks quoted in the assistant's prompt (availability around 99%, 4G median
throughput in the mid-30s Mbps, prepaid churn near 3% a month) are
order-of-magnitude figures typical of the industry, used so the assistant can say
whether a number is good. They are not sourced from any operator's published
results.
